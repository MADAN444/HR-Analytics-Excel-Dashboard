# Microsoft HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based HR Analytics Dashboard designed to provide a clear view of employee attrition and workforce patterns.

The dashboard summarizes key HR metrics and visualizes attrition across job levels, departments, age groups, overtime status, and business travel. It helps HR teams and business stakeholders quickly understand workforce trends and identify areas that may require attention.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition.
- Understand attrition patterns across different job levels.
- Compare attrition rates between departments.
- Identify age groups with higher attrition.
- Examine the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.
- Analyze the impact of business travel on attrition.
- Present HR insights through an easy-to-understand dashboard.

### Visualizations Included

- **Attrition by Job Level** – Shows the attrition rate across job levels 1 to 5.
- **Attrition by Department** – Compares attrition across Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales.
- **Attrition Trend by Age** – Shows how attrition varies across different age groups.
- **Overtime Status** – Shows attrition distribution based on overtime status.
- **Business Travel Impact** – Highlights attrition patterns associated with business travel.
- **Department Filters** – Allows users to explore the dashboard by department.
